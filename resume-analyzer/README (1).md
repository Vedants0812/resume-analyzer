# 📄 Resume Analyzer

A smart resume analyzer built with **pure JavaScript** — no paid API, no backend.

Paste your resume text and get:
- ✅ A score out of 100
- 📊 A breakdown across 6 categories
- 💡 Specific improvement suggestions
- 🔍 Keyword gap analysis

---

## 🧠 How the Scoring Works

| Category     | Max Points | What's Checked                            |
|--------------|------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Contact Info | 15         | Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Phone            |
| Summary      | 15         | Has summary section + adequate length     |
| Skills       | 20         | Skills section + keyword count            |
| Experience   | 25         | Action verbs + measurable results         |
| Education    | 10         | Education section present                 |
| Length       | 15         | Word count (100 / 250 / 450+ thresholds)  |
